# Research notes

I'm studying results from Notre Dame College's HSC 2026 batch. What started as a question about one bad mathematics prediction has turned into a much bigger job of going through old report cards.

**October 31, 2026**

I've received records from 35 students. The marks have come in different forms: photos of report cards, partial histories, and notes copied from paper. I've been putting them into a consistent format so I can compare the same subject and quiz round across students.

One thing I had to sort out early was how NDC calculates its report-card totals. The Sent-Up results include marks from earlier assessments, so they aren't three independent exams. I'm keeping the individual quiz and exam marks separate from those weighted totals.

I'm also being careful about what the comparisons can show. A lower class average might be related to a harder paper, but that isn't something I can prove from these records alone. And a student's standing among the people who shared their marks isn't the same as their official NDC position.

The data is still being prepared, so I haven't posted any findings. I want to compare changes in raw marks with changes in relative standing using the same students in both assessments, then see where the two tell different stories.

The report cards and student-level spreadsheet are private. I have a separate working copy for software tests, but generated example records aren't research observations.
